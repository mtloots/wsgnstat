# wsgnstat: The Wrapped Skew Generalised Normal Distribution

The skew generalised normal distribution on the line, constructed as a
scale mixture of a uniform and a generalised gamma variable, and its
wrapped version on the circle, with density, distribution and quantile
functions, characteristic function, trigonometric moments, random
generation, and estimation by the method of trigonometric moments
(minimum distance, with sandwich standard errors) and by maximum
likelihood. The characteristic function is evaluated through absolutely
convergent integrals (the Fox H-function route), so every routine is
available for all positive shape parameters, not only where the
Fox-Wright series converges. A pure-C back-end does the numerics and
also backs the 'Python' package 'wsgnstat'.
